# tidal architecture

tidal is only the subconscious and below-subconscious memory layer. Turn-taking, speaking decisions, and multimodal perception belong to the brain layer (growbrain). tidal only supplies memory signals.

The principle is human-likeness over usefulness. A mechanism stays when it reproduces a human memory effect, including a partial or fuzzy one. It is not tuned to maximize usefulness after the held-out bank is seen. Each mechanism names a neuroscience paper. Where a preregistration read that paper, the sentence says so. The other papers are the sources of the effect. Their measurements are not the fit numbers.

Memory vectors are text-only and modality-agnostic. `LLMMemory` encodes a text fact with SmolLM2-135M and stores the residuals. It does not read image, audio, video, or metadata features. Each stored slot has `modality`, and every write sets it to `text`.

## Store and inject

`EpisodicStore` (`tidal/episodic.py`) keeps one slot per fact: key, value, identity. Same-identity overwrite replaces the slot. Capacity 256 evicts FIFO. Key cosine does not merge a different identity. A different identity merges only when the group matches, the relation is the same non-empty string, and the surface token Jaccard is at least 0.85 (`REPEAT_JACCARD`). Chat and noise are not written.

`LLMMemory` (`tidal/llm_memory.py`) reuses that store. The default checkpoint is `HuggingFaceTB/SmolLM2-135M`. The inject layer is `n_layers // 2` (layer 15, hidden 576) and loads `tidal/llm_inject_smollm2_135m.pt`. The prompt does not contain the fact. The value is an answer-span pool at the inject layer, not L2-normalized. A phone of at least seven digits concatenates the leading coordinates of the last four answer tokens. A birthday in the spelling `Month D, YYYY` concatenates the month token, the day-tens token, the day-ones token, and the four year-digit tokens. The key drops sink tokens (norm above 8 times the median token norm of that string) and subtracts the fixed center in `tidal/llm_key_center.npz`.

With one slot the residual is

`s * u * gain * (scale(v) + MLP(scale(v)))`

`scale` is 1. `gain = 1 + 2 tanh(w · normalize(k))`. `u = 1 + tanh(valence)` at span 1. Generation does not sum a mixture onto every position. The cue is the per-token hidden state at layer 15. One slot with cosine at least theta 0.32 wins, ranked by `(log strength, cosine, -index)`. The residual on that token is `lambda * a * s * c * u * gain * (scale(v) + MLP(scale(v)))`, with `a = a_trig * fan_factor * decay` and `lambda` 0.3. `lambda` is not part of the exported activation. `fan_factor` is `max(0, (S - ln n) / S)` with `S` 1.45. It scales activation down and does not clear a trigger. On a later token, activation below 0.05 still clears the carried trace. The half-life is 30 tokens. `activation_weights` still computes the earlier top-`MIX_K = 4` mixture at temperature 0.05. Generation does not call it. Retrieval-induced forgetting follows the triggered winner. Spelling reconstructs that winner from its residue, strength, age, and valence, and does not read the stored answer string. `generate` does not read the store's `soft_k` or `soft_temp`.

Teyler and DiScenna (1986) describe the hippocampus as an index that reinstates a cortical pattern. The residual added into the language model is that idea: the stored pattern comes back into the network that is about to continue. https://doi.org/10.1037/0735-7044.100.2.147

## Mechanisms

Constants were chosen on a fit bank and were not retuned after the held-out score.

**Mixture.** `activation_weights` can leave several slots partly active. The weight is the analogue of Lebiere's chunk-choice softmax on `log(strength) + cosine`, and the sum of composed residuals is the Euclidean case of his estimation equation. Generation does not use that sum. It injects one cue winner. The preregistration read Collins and Loftus (1975) on spreading activation that summates from more than one node, McClelland and Rumelhart (1981) on parallel activation, and Lebiere (1999). https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-295X.82.6.407 https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-295X.88.5.375 https://doi.org/10.1007/s001970050071

**Coexist.** Similar memories stay in separate slots and then interfere. The preregistration read Norman and O'Reilly's preprint of the complementary-learning-systems model for pattern-separated traces, Underwood (1957) for similarity-dependent interference, and Anderson (1974) for a larger same-relation set lowering the target. The published Norman and O'Reilly paper is Psychological Review 110(4):611–646. https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-295X.110.4.611 https://doi.org/10.1037/h0044616 https://doi.org/10.1016/0010-0285(74)90021-8

**Retrieval-induced forgetting.** After a strict cue win, every other same-relation candidate that cleared theta is scaled by `1 - 0.05 * weight`. RIF runs once per cue retrieval event, not per token. A retrieval event is a trigger onset: the first token where a winner fires after no open retrieval for that memory, or a winner change. Retriggers of the same winner within its active decay window do not rerun competition. The retrieval stays open on a retrigger of that winner, including when activation is 0, and it stays open on a later token only while the carried state has memory_id >= 0 and activation > 0. A different relation is never scaled. Similarity is key cosine to the winner, so the closest companion gets weight 1 and a farther companion is scaled less. The winner is unchanged. A slot whose salience reaches 0.5 is exempt. A tie, gap 0, does not compete. Anderson, Bjork, and Bjork (1994) treat retrieval-induced forgetting as a consequence of a retrieval attempt. The preregistration also read Anderson (2003) and Norman, Newman, and Detre (2007). The human result is a partial drop in recall. The gate is that partial drop in logprob margin. Cue-independent suppression is not implemented. https://doi.org/10.1037/0278-7393.20.5.1063 https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jml.2003.08.006 https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-295X.114.4.887

**Valence.** A write's experience field adds its sign times 0.25 to a scalar on the slot. The text is not scored. `u = 1 + tanh(valence)`. Replay multiplies a nonzero valence by 0.5. Paton, Belova, Morrison, and Salzman (2006) show amygdala neurons that represent the positive and negative value of stimuli during learning. The signed gain is that distinction. The fit did not use their firing rates. https://doi.org/10.1038/nature04490

**Circadian clock.** One global angle sits under the store. `phase = sin(angle)`. Angle 0 leaves write strength at 1 and decay at `0.5 ** age`. A nonzero phase scales the write by `1 + 0.25 * phase` and the decay exponent by `1 - 0.25 * phase`. The angle is not read from fact text. `remember` does not tick. The tick is `pi/16`. Hastings, Maywood, and Brancaccio (2018) review the suprachiasmatic nucleus as a content-free circadian generator. This clock is that kind of oscillator. It is not a model of the nucleus. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41583-018-0026-z

**Relative surprise.** Surprise is the answer-token NLL already returned by the value forward. After 32 accepted surprises are stored, a salient fact is written when its surprise is at least the quantile of that window. Quantile 0, the window minimum, is the shipped point. Fewer than 32 accepted surprises leaves the salient fact accepted. A rejection does not enter the window. Lisman and Grace (2005) describe a hippocampal–VTA loop that uses novelty to control entry into long-term memory. The gate is that kind of write filter. It is not their circuit. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2005.05.002

**Replay.** Every 32 accepted writes, strength is recomputed as `0.5 ** age`, the single highest-salience slot is refreshed to strength 1, and at most one same-relation surface repeat is merged. Salience reuses the polarity vector. Wilson and McNaughton (1994) recorded hippocampal ensemble reactivation during sleep. The refresh is that kind of reactivation on a fixed budget. It is not a sleep model. Process S and offline reconsolidation are separate. https://doi.org/10.1126/science.8036517

**Spell.** When the prompt is completing the winning slot's relation, phone and birthday generation reconstructs a spelling from that slot's residue and from its strength, age, and valence. The residue is the parsed parts, built when the slot is written. It is not the raw string. `answer` stays on the slot for the reconsolidation identity check and is not the recall source. It does not spell a trained prefix and it does not spell a digit readout. The value vector is unchanged.

Exact reassembly requires strength at least 0.8, age at most 0, and absolute valence strictly below 0.75. Strength below 0.4, or age at least 4, replaces the peripheral field. Every other finite triple slips that field's order. Phone gist is `+1`, the area, and the exchange. The line is peripheral: an order slip swaps its last two digits, or adds one to the last digit when those two match; a loss adds 1111 modulo 10000. Birthday gist is the month and the year. The day is peripheral: an order slip swaps its digits when the result is a different day in 1–27, otherwise it steps to the neighbor inside that range; a loss sets the day to 1, or to 2 when it was already 1. Age is the store's consolidation counter. It is not wall time and it is not the circadian angle. The closed phone and date vocabulary has no synonym, so those two errors are the gist-preserving errors. These thresholds and the gist split are a hypothesis. No grid was run.

Bartlett, with Kintsch on the Crossref record of the 1995 reprint, describes memory as a process of reconstruction. Subjects who retold a story changed details. https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9780511759185 Brewer and Treyens (1981), Cognitive Psychology, issued 1981-04. Role of schemata in memory for places. Crossref recorded no abstract on 2026-10-24, so this citation is the title. https://doi.org/10.1016/0010-0285(81)90008-6 Kensinger (2009), Emotion Review, issued 2009-04. Remembering the Details: Effects of Emotion. The abstract says emotion does not enhance memory equally for all aspects of an experience, and it describes arousal's focal enhancements, more often for negative than for positive experiences. https://doi.org/10.1177/1754073908100432 The mapping from that focal account onto a kept gist and a lost or reordered peripheral field is a hypothesis. It is not a claim that the abstract says peripheral details are lost. [#46](https://github.com/yunshenya/tidal/pull/46) remains the historical exact-copy record.

**Sleep pressure.** `LLMMemory` owns process S, a scalar `sleep_pressure` in `[0.1, 1]`. Awake time raises it: `S = 1 - (1 - S) * exp(-t / 16)`. Sleep lowers it: `S = 0.1 + (S - 0.1) * exp(-D / 4)`. A finite duration does not assign 0. If the exponential underflows, the result is the floor 0.1, which is still above 0. `store.clear` does not move it. Cue retrieval does not move it, and it is not multiplied into the residual. Daan, Beersma, and Borbély (1984) describe a sleep-regulating variable that increases during wakefulness and decreases during sleep, with circadian thresholds. https://doi.org/10.1152/ajpregu.1984.246.2.R161 Borbély (2016) states that homeostatic Process S interacts with circadian Process C. https://doi.org/10.1111/jsr.12371 Borbély (2022) reviews the model's origins. https://doi.org/10.1111/jsr.13598 Borbély (1982), Human Neurobiology, has no Crossref DOI and is not cited. The time constants and the floor are a hypothesis. Those papers' constants are in hours and were not copied into these abstract duration units. No grid was run. At that export, `interface_version` is 4.

growbrain is the planned consumer of the exported pressure. Its planned threshold is `θ = θ0 + a·S − b·C`. tidal does not compute that expression. Process C remains the exported circadian phase. There is no growbrain package and no switch.

**Offline reconsolidation.** `sleep_for` rewrites a slot only when prediction error `1 - cosine(key, value)` is above 0.25. The value becomes `(1 - 0.1) * v + 0.1 * unit(key) * ||v||`. Strength becomes `strength * exp(-0.05 * min(error, 1))` and stays positive. Sevenster, Beckers, and Kindt (2013) showed that prediction error is required before a reactivated human fear memory is updated. Retrieval without that error does not labilize the memory. https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1231357 The threshold, the blend, and the strength step are a hypothesis. This is not their propranolol protocol. The audit row is `index`, `error`, `updated`, `strength_before`, and `strength_after`. It has no fact text. `cue_fields`, `generate`, and `_rank` do not read it. Awake `remember` still stores `text` and `answer`. Repeat detection reads `text`. Reconsolidation checks that `text` and `answer` are unchanged. Spelling does not read `answer`. It reads the residue built at encode time. Replay does not write a raw string.

`offline_ticks` does not change process S. With no external cue, a tick recalls one slot when the tick index is a multiple of 16, the store is nonempty, and S is at least 0.2. The winner is the greatest `(salience, strength, -index)`. The same prediction-error rule decides whether that recall rewrites the slot. Tick 0 fires, so 16 ticks fire once. The rate is 0.0625, above 0 and below 0.10. Berntsen (2020) describes memories of personal events that come to mind with no conscious initiation of retrieval. https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2019.0693 That paper says involuntary autobiographical memory cannot be equated with mind-wandering. This tick is that no-cue recall, not mind-wandering. The period and the pressure minimum are a hypothesis. `sleep_for` also runs `floor(duration)` of these ticks after the sweep.

**Arousal.** `LLMMemory` owns an LC-NE arousal scalar in `[0, 1]`. It starts at 0.2. Quiet tokens move it by `0.2 + (level - 0.2) * exp(-1 / 8)`. A surprise raises it only when that surprise is strictly above the mean of at least two earlier samples in a separate window of 8. The bump is `0.35 * tanh((surprise - mean) / (|mean| + 1))`. `|valence|` adds `0.25 * tanh(|valence|)`. A positive trigger count adds `0.20 * tanh(count)`, and a count of 0 does not decay. Aston-Jones and Cohen (2005) describe phasic and tonic locus coeruleus–norepinephrine modes. Phasic activation follows a decision outcome and is proposed to facilitate the next behavior. Tonic activity, when utility falls, is associated with disengagement. https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev.neuro.28.061604.135709 tidal does not choose exploitation or exploration, and it does not choose to speak or to wait. The quiet decay is not that tonic rise. The baseline, the time constant, the weights, and the window are a hypothesis. No grid was run.

`remember` calls the surprise update only after an accepted write has already appended the measured answer NLL, and only when that NLL is finite. A nonzero experience also calls the valence update with the slot's valence after the step. Neither call writes a slot, and neither call moves process S. The write-gate window is unchanged. `cue_fields` and `advance_cue` step after the residual is computed: one trigger bump on a triggered token, one quiet step otherwise. `_on_layer` repeats the current scalar and does not step, because generation re-forwards the prefix. Offline ticks and sleep ticks export the current scalar and do not step. `wake_for` and `sleep_for` do not move arousal. Arousal is not multiplied into the residual, and it is not an input to process S. `store.clear` does not move it. growbrain only consumes the scalar. There is no switch and no feature flag.

**Wall clock.** Cue activation has two decay clocks. The token factor stays `exp(-dt / tau)` with half-life 30 tokens. Wall seconds since the trigger contribute `exp(-elapsed / wall_tau)` with half-life 60 seconds. The remaining factor is the lower of the two. That is the stronger decay, and it is not the product. Elapsed 0 returns the token expression without taking a minimum, so a frozen wall clock is the shipped formula. `dt` counts tokens already seen after the trigger. A trace holds its wall reading fixed, so an unapplied tick is invisible inside that forward. A new winner resets `dt` and stamps the current wall time. A fresh `cue_fields` or generation forward starts empty, so time since boot does not decay a new retrieval. A continued winner never rises. Below the 0.05 floor on that decay path, the carried trace clears. Wall time moves only through `advance_wall`, whose default tick is 1 second. The code does not read the operating-system clock.

The internal clock adds `1.25 * seconds` on the same tick. `calibrate(timestamp)` adds `0.5 * (timestamp - internal)`. Decay does not read that estimate, so calibration cannot change activation, and it does not change process S or arousal. The store circadian span and step are unchanged. This seconds clock is not process C. The tick, the seconds half-life, the drift rate, and the gain are a hypothesis. No grid was run. Joordens and Becker (1997) studied short and long semantic priming in lexical decision. The token unit is not seconds. https://doi.org/10.1037/0278-7393.23.5.1083 Buhusi and Meck (2005) review functional and neural mechanisms of interval timing. https://doi.org/10.1038/nrn1764 Allman, Teki, Griffiths, and Meck (2014) review properties of the internal clock and of subjective time. https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-psych-010213-115117 Crossref recorded no abstract for the 2005 or 2014 paper on 2026-10-24, so those citations stay at the title. The drift rate is not a dopamine dose. The gain is not a circadian phase shift. The live `interface_version` is 5. No per-token clock field is added. `advance_wall` and `calibrate` return wall time, internal time, and signed `internal - wall` because those calls are how a tick and a timestamp are applied. `store.clear` does not move either reading. There is no switch and no feature flag.

## Language-model backend

tidal calls four hooks. The memory layer still chooses the winner, the fan, the decay, and the residual. The backend runs the model and adds that residual. `SmolLM2Backend` is the only implementation, and `LLMMemory` constructs it by default, including when the caller passes a model. There is no switch and no environment flag. The growbrain backend is **planned**. This repository does not import growbrain.

`encode_hidden(text) -> (hidden, encoding, logits)` is one forward. `hidden` is the inject-layer residual, float32 `[T, H]`, and `logits` are from that same forward. It does not add a memory residual. `encode_cue(text) -> cue` returns the pre-add inject-layer rows, float64 `[T, H]`. It does not inject and does not change the store. `encode_key_hidden(text) -> (hidden, encoding)` is the key-span form of that encode. When the stack matches `LlamaModel.forward`, it runs the decoder prefix through the inject layer and restores `num_hidden_layers` before returning. Otherwise it calls `encode_hidden` and drops the logits. The rows match `encode_hidden`. `answer_nll(logits, token_ids, answer_mask) -> float` is the total answer-token NLL, in nats, from those logits. It does not run the model. Position `i` is scored from `logits[i - 1]`. The value encode and this NLL share the one `encode_hidden` forward. `inject_residual(output, residual, *, broadcast=False, batched=True)` adds a residual into a decoder-layer output. `broadcast=False` adds a numpy residual `[batch, steps, hidden]` at the same index, in the layer output's dtype and device. `broadcast=True` adds one vector on every position, and only teacher-forced loss uses it. When the retrieval residual is all zeros, the SmolLM2 hook returns None and does not call inject, so the layer output object stays the one the block produced.

## Signals exported to growbrain

The per-token cue export is what generation injects. The growbrain backend that would implement the hooks above is **planned**. The repository still has no growbrain package. Each exported token carries `memory_id`, `vec`, `activation = a_trig * fan_factor * decay`, `strength*confidence`, `valence` as `u - 1`, `circadian`, `triggered`, `modality`, `sleep_pressure`, `source`, `arousal`, and `interface_version`. `modality` is `text` on every token, including a cleared trace. `sleep_pressure` is process S, float32 `[1, T]`, the same value on every token of the call. `source` is `cue`, `spontaneous`, or `none`. The cue path emits `cue` or `none`. `spontaneous` is an offline tick, or a sleep tick, with no external cue. `arousal` is the LC-NE scalar in `[0, 1]`, float32 `[1, T]`. `cue_fields` and `advance_cue` export the level after each token's step. `_on_layer`, an offline tick, and a sleep tick repeat the current scalar and do not step. The live `interface_version` is 5. Version 2 was the activation formula. Version 3 adds `modality`. Version 4 adds `sleep_pressure` and `source`. Version 5 adds `arousal`. There is no perceptual-fingerprint field and no clock-error field. `decay` is the token factor when wall elapsed is 0, and otherwise the lower of the token factor and the wall-second factor. The drifted internal clock stays inside `advance_wall` and `calibrate`. `u - 1` is the offset in `u = 1 + tanh(valence)`, so a valence of 0 exports 0. growbrain only consumes `arousal`. There is no switch.

`fan_factor` decreases with the number of memories the cue retrieves. Anderson and Reder (1999) write source activation as `S - ln(fan)`. https://doi.org/10.1037/0096-3445.128.2.186 That factor slows or weakens retrieval. It does not abolish a trigger. The fast `decay` is a per-token timescale. Joordens and Becker (1997) found semantic priming reduced to non-significant levels after one or two intervening items. Their unit is items. A conversion from items to tokens is a hypothesis. https://doi.org/10.1037/0278-7393.23.5.1083 The slow timescale is the strength and replay state already in the store. Tulving, Schacter, and Stark (1982) found word-fragment priming unchanged from one hour to seven days while recognition fell. https://doi.org/10.1037/0278-7393.8.4.336

The cue itself is the planned reason a memory is retrieved. Tulving and Thomson (1973) state encoding specificity: retrieval depends on the cue that was encoded with the episode. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0020071 Vaz, Inati, Brunel, and Zaghloul (2019) recorded coupled ripples between the medial temporal lobe and neocortex during human memory retrieval. https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aau8956 Lu, Hasson, and Norman (2022) model when an episodic memory is retrieved and when a new one is encoded. https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.74445

## Multimodal fusion division (PLANNED, after text stage-1; no code now)

This split is planned. It comes after text stage-1. There is no code for it, and it adds no interface fields. The references below have not yet been re-verified by tidal.

tidal, the subconscious layer, would do only low-level automatic fusion: a temporal window, spatial or source matching, and reliability weighting. Cross-sensory events that occur together would raise salience and trigger a direct report upward (Meredith and Stein 1983; Van der Burg 2008). Already-learned pairs would complete through concept vectors (Faivre 2014). Cross-modal pairs would replay during sleep (Rudoy 2009). The fusion window and the weights would be learned from co-occurrence (Wallace and Stein 2007).

The brain layer (growbrain) would form new pairings and semantic-level fusion, including lip with voice and text with image. That needs attention (Palmer and Ramsey 2012; Alsius 2005; Talsma 2010). Pairings it learns would be written into tidal. growbrain may modulate tidal's fusion weights, in the way cortex modulates the superior colliculus.

Hypothesis, on weak evidence: the amygdala does fast cross-modal affect tagging.
